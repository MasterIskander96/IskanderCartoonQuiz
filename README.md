# IskanderCartoonQuiz 🎬

A fun console quiz that tests your knowledge of three popular cartoons: **The Lion King**, **SpongeBob SquarePants**, and **Tom and Jerry**.

## 📌 About the Project

**IskanderCartoonQuiz** is a simple programming project where the player answers questions about popular cartoons.

The quiz includes questions about **The Lion King**, **SpongeBob SquarePants**, and **Tom and Jerry**. The player chooses the correct answer and receives points for each correct response.

## 🎮 Features

* 🦁 Questions about **The Lion King**
* 🧽 Questions about **SpongeBob SquarePants**
* 🐱 Questions about **Tom and Jerry**
* ❓ Multiple-choice questions
* ✅ Checks the player's answers
* 🏆 Counts the player's score
* 📊 Shows the final result
* 🎯 Simple and fun gameplay

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Start the program.
2. Read the cartoon question.
3. Choose the correct answer.
4. Continue answering the questions.
5. Get points for correct answers.
6. Finish the quiz and check your final score.
7. Try again and improve your result!

## 🎬 Cartoons

### 🦁 The Lion King

Questions about characters, events, and the story of **The Lion King**.

### 🧽 SpongeBob SquarePants

Questions about characters, places, and events from **SpongeBob SquarePants**.

### 🐱 Tom and Jerry

Questions about the characters and adventures of **Tom and Jerry**.
